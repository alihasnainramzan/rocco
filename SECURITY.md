## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and integrity of Rocco Master Panel seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability or concern within this repository:

1. **Do not create a public issue.**
2. Email your findings directly to our security and legal team:
   - Creator Contact: [raceducate@gmail.com](mailto:raceducate@gmail.com)
   - Legal Counsel: [alihasnainentrepreneur@gmail.com](mailto:alihasnainentrepreneur@gmail.com)
3. Please include details regarding the environment, version, and steps to reproduce. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours.
