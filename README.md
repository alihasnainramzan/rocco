## 🌟 What is Rocco Master Panel?

**Rocco Master Panel** is an ultra-fast studio toolkit designed directly for **Adobe Animate CC (2021 to 2026)**. Built to speed up production timelines by up to **80%**, it equips animators with 33 dedicated tools, procedural puppet rigging, AI automatic speech-to-mouth lip-syncing, bitmap-to-vector line tracing, and a cloud-powered marketplace for free vector character rigs, backgrounds, and prop packs.

Best of all: **No sign-ups, no logins, and no account barriers.** Anyone can download the installer and begin animating immediately.

---

## ⚡ Key Studio Features

### 👤 1. Character & Puppet Rigging Pro
- **1-Click Smart Auto-Rigging**: Instantly detects vector line limbs, calculates joint intersections, and creates nested hierarchy symbols with pre-positioned pivot points.
- **Joint Line Cutter Tool**: Slice hand-drawn grouped artwork along any line stroke into clean, independent joints ready for symbol conversion.
- **Smart Locomotion Engine**: Procedural walk cycles, athletic runs, jumps, and physics-based follow-through across timeline spans.

### 🎙️ 2. AI Lip-Sync Engine
- **Automated Phoneme Mapping**: Powered by the Rhubarb speech engine. Analyzes WAV/MP3 voice tracks and automatically assigns phoneme mouth shapes (`AI`, `E`, `O`, `U`, `MBP`, `L`, `ETC`, `REST`) directly onto your character timeline layers.

### 🎨 3. Vector Stroke Tracer Pro & AI Gap Healer
- **Raster to Clean Vector Strokes**: Convert rough storyboard scans, sketches, and bitmap artwork (PNG/JPG) into crisp, editable stroke lines within Animate CC.
- **AI Vector Ray-Marching Gap Healer**: Automatically finds broken line art gaps and heals them along natural Bézier curves so you can use the Paint Bucket Tool without leaks.

### 👁️ 4. Auto Eyeblink Inserter & Talking Loops
- Distributes natural, randomized eye blink keyframes across your timeline with customizable frame spacing.
- Procedural talking mouth animations for secondary/background crowd characters.

### 📐 5. Timeline Architect & Optimizer
- **Bulk Frame Extender**: Uniformly extend or contract keyframe durations across 50+ timeline layers in 1 click.
- **Keyframe Gap Equalizer**: Spreads keyframe intervals smoothly without breaking existing classic tweens.
- **Symbol & Layer Renamer**: Mass-renames library graphics, movie clips, and stage layers with custom prefixes.

### ☁️ 6. Cloudflare R2 & Firebase Cloud Marketplace
- Direct access to free character rigs, props, and background stages.
- Delivered at zero latency via **Cloudflare R2** and Google **Firebase Firestore**.

## 📥 Quick 3-Step Installation

1. **Download the Setup Wizard**:
   - Download [`Rocco_MasterPanel_v1.0.2_Setup.exe`](https://github.com/alihasnainramzan/rocco/releases/download/v1.0.2/Rocco_MasterPanel_v1.0.2_Setup.exe) from the Releases section.
2. **Run Installer**:
   - Double-click `Rocco_MasterPanel_v1.0.2_Setup.exe`.
   - The installer automatically detects your installed Adobe Animate versions (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026) and configures the panel in one click.
3. **Open Inside Adobe Animate**:
   - Launch Adobe Animate CC.
   - Navigate to: **Window &rarr; Extensions &rarr; Rocco Master Panel**.
   - Start animating!
